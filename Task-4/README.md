The Executive Business Insights Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance using the Superstore dataset. It enables users to monitor key performance indicators, evaluate customer and product performance, and analyze sales across different regions.
Key Insights:
The business generated ₹2.30M in total sales with an overall profit of ₹286.40K.
The dashboard tracks approximately 5K orders from 793 unique customers, offering insights into customer reach and order volume.
The Top 10 Customers by Sales visual identifies the highest-value customers, helping prioritize customer relationship strategies.
The Sales by State map highlights geographical sales distribution, making it easier to identify high-performing markets.
The Orders by Ship Mode chart shows that Standard Class is the most frequently used shipping method, followed by Second Class and First Class.
The Sales by Sub-Category chart reveals the best-performing product sub-categories, supporting inventory planning and product strategy.
Interactive slicers for Year, Category, and Region allow users to filter the dashboard dynamically and explore business performance from different perspectives.
